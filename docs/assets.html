Designs, sheets and environments

Start with the original design, then follow its derived sheet. The complete design history stays available behind the filter.

Krea created the original designs and environments. I used GPT-image 2 for the character sheets through Codex image generation. The two newer Foideldore sheets explicitly requested gpt-image-2-medium.

Each recipe separates the original design prompt from the sheet-generation prompt and records reference roles. Images are the native files, with no crop, resize or padding.

Episode 2’s actual office is the Office v2 second reroll, not the earlier office-v1 image. Both are retained with their own exact prompts. The actual Retro Anime inspired moodboard by smoer is shared on Krea; see its preview and how I used it.
