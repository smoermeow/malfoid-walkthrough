Generation walkthrough

Follow the example

Choose a section and follow its real dependency chain rather than treating the full episode as one giant prompt.

A short path through the example

  1. Watch a finished episode on the overview page.
  2. Open its episode walkthrough and choose a production section.
  3. Inspect the exact original designs and derived sheets linked from that render’s reference list. Start Harry’s path with the original design, then its sheet; do the same for Foideldore.
  4. Download the complete relevant prompt skill from the catalogue. Load its required knowledge before using it.
  5. Download the render’s prompt and native reference files. Match reference order and roles, including any video tail or source frame. A source-frame reference and a character sheet do different jobs.
  6. Use your own provider account and explicitly approve any paid generation. The recorded example uses Seedance 2.5 at 480p, without upscaling.
  7. When continuing, use the preceding clip and exact selected source inputs documented for that version. Do not replace an earlier source with a different reroll merely because it has a similar name.
  8. Keep the returned native video and its generated dialogue/SFX. The creator assembled these clips in CapCut. Episode 1 adds Walked Away; Episode 2 has no added music.

What is available to browse

The site, still images, prompt text, ordered inputs, finished videos and skill downloads can be browsed without a generation account. The full local candidate includes native media, not cropped thumbnails or regenerated substitutes.

What is needed to generate

Use your own agent/model access, Krea and Dreamina access as applicable, and the required provider software. The Dreamina wrapper depends on an external dreamina-canvas CLI. Credentials, OAuth state and paid credits are not included. No command on this page automatically launches a generation.

The still-image history uses Krea 2 Turbo (krea/krea-2/medium-turbo). Earlier Codex CLI sheets do not disclose their exact image-model version. The newer Foideldore sheets explicitly requested gpt-image-2-medium; hood-down returned low reported quality and hood-up medium. Those differences remain recorded.

Reproduction limits

Seeds were not recorded. The historical moodboard identifier and strength are retained, but its contents are not bundled. Provider/backend versions and current skill snapshots may differ from what ran historically. These are recipes for understanding and following the process; they do not promise pixel-identical regeneration.

Episode 1 Prompt 1 and Prompt 2 have recovered authoring revisions and creator-identified outputs, without a receipt proving the exact submitted revision. This does not change the creator’s confirmed model/resolution or the use of skill-written prompts. Other exact submissions are provided where recovered.
