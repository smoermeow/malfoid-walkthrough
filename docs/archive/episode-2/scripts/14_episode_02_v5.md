# Global visual style

Retro anime, bold linework, Shirow meets Frieren. Mature proportions, detailed hand-drawn environments, expressive character acting, graphic silhouettes, selective perspective exaggeration, and strong anime impact frames only at key moments.

# Voice anchors

**Male 1:** Young male voice, clear and natural, slightly roughened by exhaustion, emotionally direct, with a grounded mid-range tone and restrained intensity.

**Malfoid:** Young female voice, smooth and slightly low-pitched, precise articulation and understated confidence. Her guarded spoken delivery has a dry, lightly teasing edge. The intrusive handsome thought is soft, dreamy and genuinely affectionate internal voiceover; her brisk, emphatic internal self-rebuke begins after the headshake.

**Foideldore:** Mature female voice, grounded and conversational, slightly low and warm, with a subtle husky texture, clear and confident with natural authority, dry humor and emotional weight. Not aristocratic, theatrical, overly refined, or polished.

# Global lighting

Cold blue moonlight enters through the tall arched tower windows, contrasted by warm amber light from candles, lamps, and magical fixtures throughout Foideldore’s office. Strong rim light defines silhouettes around the windows while warm interior light shapes the characters and detailed hand-drawn environment.

# Plot summary

Male 1 and Malfoid stand before Foideldore after the events in the ruined courtyard. Male 1 argues that Malfoid cannot be sent to Azkaban after saving him, Male 2, and Female 1. Foideldore initially faces him near the windows, then turns away and remains silently looking outside while Male 1 continues arguing.

Malfoid watches Male 1 rant with irritation. The intrusive thought that he is handsome draws her into a genuinely dreamy, affectionate state and makes her blush. She interrupts the thought with a rapid headshake. After the shake, she thinks her refusal, recovers her defensive annoyance and snaps at Male 1.

Their argument escalates while Foideldore’s frustration builds. Foideldore finally turns, crosses to the desk, and slams both hands down. After stopping their argument, she warns Male 1 that the Ministry may not be persuaded so easily, and that if they cannot be reasoned with, the situation may eventually move beyond her ability to prevent.

# Scene blocking

From the visitor side facing the windows, Male 1 stands left and Malfoid right, together on the visitor side of the desk. Foideldore stays alone on the opposite, window side. The full width of the desk separates Foideldore from both students throughout; neither student walks around it. Camera reversals do not change these physical positions.

## SHOT 16

**Duration:** 6.0s

**Camera:** Slight low-angle medium-wide shot from behind Male 1 and Malfoid, looking across the desk toward Foideldore at the tall windows. Male 1 and Malfoid frame the near foreground edges. One continuous slow zoom toward Foideldore.

**Action:** Foideldore remains facing the windows with her back toward the room, her fist already clenched at her side.

The camera slowly closes in.

Her shoulders tighten further. Her posture becomes rigid. Her head lowers slightly.

As the zoom continues, Male 1 and Malfoid gradually disappear from the edges of frame and Foideldore dominates the composition.

At the peak of the tension, Foideldore suddenly turns toward them.

Because the camera is already positioned slightly low, she immediately becomes imposing as she turns. She strides toward the desk, growing rapidly larger in frame.

On impact, focus holds on her hands in the near foreground as books, parchment, ink bottles, and small brass objects jump and rattle.

The focus then racks from her planted hands up to her face.

Foideldore glares directly toward Male 1 and Malfoid.

**Dialogue:**  
**Foideldore:** “ENOUGH!”

**SFX:** Quick footsteps toward the desk. Heavy desk impact. Books, parchment, ink bottles, and small desk objects jump and rattle.

**Lighting:** Cold moonlight creates a strong rim around Foideldore at the windows. As she approaches the desk, warm interior light increasingly catches her face. After the impact, her hands are strongly defined in the foreground before the rack focus settles on her face.
