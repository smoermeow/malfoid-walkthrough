Continuity: Preserve Male 1's bruises and cheek cut. The mouth-corner blood is visibly wiped in Shot 16 and must not return afterward. Both protagonists retain their wands in their right hands. Preserve Male 1 frame-left and Malfoid frame-right across the intimate profile shots.
Critical constraint: Shot 11 is exactly the specified two-second ground-level collapse. No alternative fall, disintegration or additional action. The Evil Lord never completes his curse. The followers' defeat is revealed, not explained. No kiss, tenderness, added combat or extra dialogue. Reference display poses never replace staging.
