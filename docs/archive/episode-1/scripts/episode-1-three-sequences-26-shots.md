## Global visual style
Retro anime, bold linework, Shirow meets Frieren. Mature proportions, detailed hand-drawn environments, restrained facial acting, graphic silhouettes, selective perspective exaggeration, and strong anime impact frames only at key moments.

## Voice anchors

**Evil Lord:** Low male voice, smooth and controlled, aristocratic and precise, restrained rather than gravelly, with a cold resonant presence and deliberate pacing.

**Male 1:** Young male voice, clear and natural, slightly roughened by exhaustion, emotionally direct, with a grounded mid-range tone and restrained intensity.

**Malfoid:** Young female voice, cool and composed, smooth and slightly low-pitched, precise articulation, understated confidence, with a dry, lightly teasing edge rather than softness.

## Global lighting
Cold blue moonlight dominates the ruined castle courtyard, broken by weak orange fires and drifting embers. Hard shadows and rim light create strong silhouettes through the smoke.

## SHOT 10

**Duration:** 4.5s

**Camera:** Tight frontal medium close-up on the Evil Lord from Male 1’s eyeline; static.

**Action:** The Evil Lord begins the killing curse. On “Avada—”, Malfoid suddenly blitzes into frame directly behind him in a teleport-like anime burst, already holding her wand to his temple; the Evil Lord freezes, realizes who is behind him, and Malfoid immediately fires.

**Dialogue:**  
**EVIL LORD: voice (suddenly forceful and lethal, all previous restraint snapping into the attack):** 「アバダ――」  
**EVIL LORD: voice (very quiet, breath caught slightly, disbelief entering the voice):** 「……ドラコ。」  
**MALFOID: voice (calm and absolute, level volume, no hesitation or anger):** 「アバダ・ケダブラ。」

**SFX:** Sharp blitz impact, wand tap against skin, point-blank curse discharge.

**Lighting:** Cold moonlight on the Evil Lord; Malfoid snaps into a hard silver rim behind him before the frame floods green.

**VFX:** High-speed smear/blitz entry, brief held frame on the reveal, then green impact flash and shadow inversion.
