# Global visual style
Retro anime, bold linework, Shirow meets Frieren. Mature proportions, detailed hand-drawn environments, restrained facial acting, graphic silhouettes, selective perspective exaggeration, and strong anime impact frames only at key moments.

# Global lighting
Cold blue moonlight dominates the ruined Hogwarts courtyard, broken by weak orange fires and drifting embers. Hard shadows and rim light create strong silhouettes through the smoke.

# Plot summary
Harry, Ron, and Hermione have been cornered in the ruined Hogwarts courtyard by Voldemort and a ring of Death Eaters, with Malfoy seemingly standing among Voldemort’s followers. Voldemort approaches the injured Harry, taunts him, then theatrically turns away to address his followers before returning his attention to Harry. Malfoy silently ends up directly behind Voldemort and presses her wand against his temple as he prepares to kill Harry. She kills Voldemort at point-blank range. A wider view then reveals that the Death Eaters who had surrounded them are already down. Malfoy approaches the stunned Harry, deliberately leaves their relationship unresolved, rejects Lucius when he reaches for her, and walks away alone.
